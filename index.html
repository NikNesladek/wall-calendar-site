Wall Calendar

A small Android app that turns a tablet on the wall into a family calendar.

Wall Calendar shows a Google Calendar in a month view and a day view, kept in sync both ways: events added on a phone appear on the wall, and changes made on the wall appear on the phone. A sidebar lists unscheduled tasks from a Google Tasks list, which can be dragged onto a day to schedule them.

Who it's for

Wall Calendar is a private app built for one family. It isn't sold, advertised or available in any app store, and it has no accounts of its own: it signs in with the Google account of the person whose calendar it shows.

How it handles your data

Your calendar and tasks travel only between Google and the tablet. There is no Wall Calendar server, and the developer never receives your data. The privacy policy has the details.
