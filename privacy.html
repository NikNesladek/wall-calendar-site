Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Wall Calendar is a private Android app that shows a Google Calendar and a Google Tasks list on a wall-mounted tablet. This policy explains what Google data it uses and what happens to it.

What the app accesses

When you connect your Google account, you are asked to allow:

  • Your list of calendars (read-only), so you can choose which ones appear on the wall.
  • Events on your calendars, to show them and to let you add, edit and delete events from the tablet. Calendars shared with you as view-only stay read-only.
  • Google Tasks, to show open, undated tasks from a list named "Wall Inbox" (created by the app if it doesn't exist), to add tasks to it, and to mark a task complete when you schedule it on the calendar.

The app does not access your email, contacts, photos, files or any other Google data.

How it's used

This data is used only to display and edit your calendar and tasks on the tablet. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or training any model.

Where it's stored

Data goes directly between Google and the tablet over encrypted connections. There is no Wall Calendar server: the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

To start quickly and to keep working during a Wi-Fi outage, the app keeps a copy of recently shown events and tasks in its private storage on the tablet. This copy is excluded from device backups. Sign-in is handled by Google Play services on the device; the app does not store passwords or long-lived sign-in tokens.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. Nothing is sent to any party other than Google.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In the app, open Settings → Disconnect Google account. This removes the app's access at Google and deletes everything the app saved on the tablet.
  • From any device, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove "Wall Calendar". The app then signs out and deletes its saved copy the next time it syncs.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes its saved data from the tablet.

Events created from the tablet are ordinary Google Calendar events and stay in your calendar until you delete them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Wall Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Wall Calendar is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
